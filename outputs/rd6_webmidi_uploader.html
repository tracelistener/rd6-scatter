RD-6 Scatter Updater

Install Scatter on your Behringer RD-6, back up the firmware it has now, or go back to the official firmware.

Before you start: use Chrome or Edge on a computer, and close SynthTribe and other music apps. While an update runs, keep the USB cable connected and keep this tab open and in front. Scatter is unofficial, experimental firmware.

1 Connect your RD-6

Connect the RD-6 to this computer with a USB cable, switch it on and press STOP. Then click Connect. If the browser asks to let this page control your MIDI devices, choose Allow.

Firmware

The RD-6 can't send its firmware to a computer. Instead, the updater saves Behringer's official file for the version your RD-6 reports. Restoring that file puts the same firmware back.
