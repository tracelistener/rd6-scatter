RD-6 WebMIDI Uploader

Download official stock updates, save a restore copy, prepare Scatter locally and update over USB. Transfer protocol: one frame in flight, the exact expected reply per frame, a 2 s timeout, no automatic retry, and an abort on any unexpected reply.

This writes experimental firmware. Stock 1.0.6 restoration was tested on one working RD-6. This tool cannot read a full-flash backup; restoration requires a working update interface. Keep USB and power connected, keep this tab in the foreground, and close SynthTribe and other MIDI apps before you start.
